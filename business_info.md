## 5. Core Services & Photography Offerings

1. **Luxury Wedding Day Coverage:**
   * **Mehndi / Mayun / Sangeet:** Candid colors, energetic dances, ambient low-light coverage, slow-motion reels.
   * **Barat:** High-impact entrance captures, emotional Rukhsati moments, detailed jewelry and bridal couture shots.
   * **Walima / Reception:** Sophisticated editorial portraits, grand ballroom atmospheres, family legacy portraits.
   * **Nikkah Ceremonies:** Intimate, sacred moments, documentarian storytelling, signing captures.

2. **Cinematic Wedding Films:**
   * 4K Ultra-HD multicam filming with cinema lenses (Sony Alpha FX series / G-Master glass).
   * 3–5 Minute Cinematic Highlights Teaser set to curated musical scores.
   * Full-length documentary edit covering all speeches, dances, and ceremonies.
   * Aerial 4K Drone cinematography for venue scale and grand arrivals.

3. **Pre-Wedding & Editorial Couple Shoots:**
   * Creative conceptual shoots at heritage locations (Lahore Fort, Haveli Barood Khana, private royal estates).
   * Modern contemporary studio sessions with minimalist high-fashion lighting.

4. **Bridal Fashion & Commercial Campaigns:**
   * Collaborative shoots with top makeup artists, luxury salons, and bridal wear couturiers.
   * Model portfolios and product lookbooks.
